Who sees what
  • Patient: only their own results, only once signed, in plain language.
  • Clinician: every patient, the worklist, imports, outbreak analysis and technical reports.
  • Researcher: de-identified results only: week instead of date, age band, campus, no names or codes. Counts under 5 are hidden.

The database rules enforce these limits, not just this page.
